Weather Intelligence

NWS narrative text → Lakebase pgvector → semantic search

Step 1 — Sync weather documents

Harvests active alerts and forecast narratives from api.weather.gov into weather_documents.

Step 2 — Embed (run on a cluster)

The embedding model needs a cluster. Open notebooks/ingest_weather_embeddings.py in your Databricks Git folder, attach it to a cluster, and click Run All.

Step 3 — Semantic search

Cosine similarity over weather_embeddings using pgvector's <=> operator.
